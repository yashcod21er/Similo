Every photograph on the Similo website is free to use under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): commercial use without asking permission and
without attribution. We credit the photographers anyway. No Unsplash+ (paid) photos are used.

Files live in `frontend/public/images/<area>/` as WebP in two widths (`<name>-800.webp` and
`<name>-1600.webp`) and are shown with the `Photo` or `PhotoFrame` component.

## How to add a photo

1. Pick a free photo (not Unsplash+, not premium) from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) or
   [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com). Avoid visible company logos, recognisable trademarks, real
   websites on screens and children.
2. Open it and check it before using it: subject, quality, readable logos, text on walls or screens.
3. Convert to 1600 px and 800 px wide WebP (quality about 78) and add both files.
4. Add one row to the table below, and a meaningful `alt` text where it is used.

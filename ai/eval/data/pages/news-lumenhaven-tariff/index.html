Lumenhaven Energy tariff change: what it means for you

By R. Analyst, consumer team

Lumenhaven Energy has announced a change to its standard tariff from next month.

Customers of Lumenhaven Energy on the standard plan could see a small rise, while fixed-price customers are unaffected.

If you are worried about your Lumenhaven Energy bill, compare plans before the change takes effect.

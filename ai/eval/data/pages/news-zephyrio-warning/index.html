Customers warned about fake Zephyrio login pages

By S. Writer, security desk

Security researchers say fake sign-in pages that copy Zephyrio Mail are being sent to customers by text message.

Zephyrio Mail has told customers it will never ask for a password by message. Always type the address yourself and check it carefully.

If you entered your password on a suspicious page, change it straight away and tell Zephyrio Mail support.

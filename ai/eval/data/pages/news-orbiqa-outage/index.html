Orbiqa Shop outage: what happened

By D. Editor

Orbiqa Shop was unavailable for around two hours on Monday after a problem with its checkout system.

A statement from Orbiqa Shop said no customer data was affected and orders were not lost.

Shoppers on social media reported being unable to pay on Orbiqa Shop during the outage.

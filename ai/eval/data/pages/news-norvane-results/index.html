Norvane Bank reports steady quarter

By A. Reporter, business desk

Norvane Bank said on Tuesday that profits were steady in the latest quarter, helped by growth in savings accounts.

A spokesperson for Norvane Bank said the bank would open two new branches next year.

Analysts told the Ledger that Norvane Bank faces competition from newer online lenders, but customers have so far stayed loyal.

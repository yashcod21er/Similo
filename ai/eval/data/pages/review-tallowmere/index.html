Tallowmere Cloud review: is it worth it?

Our rating: 4 out of 5

We tested Tallowmere Cloud for a month to see how it compares with other storage services.

Good

  • Generous free storage
  • Simple sharing links
  • Reliable photo backup

Not so good

  • Slower uploads on mobile data
  • Fewer team features

Tallowmere Cloud is a solid choice for families and students. Heavy team users may want more tools.

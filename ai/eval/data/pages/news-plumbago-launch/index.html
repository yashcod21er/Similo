Plumbago Pay adds contactless wallet

By M. Blogger

Plumbago Pay has added tap-to-pay to its mobile wallet, letting users pay in shops with a phone.

The Plumbago Pay team said the feature will roll out gradually over the next month.

We tried Plumbago Pay in three shops and it worked every time, though one cafe terminal needed a second tap.

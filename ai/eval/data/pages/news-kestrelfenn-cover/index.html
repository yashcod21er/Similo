Kestrel & Fenn Insurance announces new flood cover

By L. Writer, local news

Kestrel & Fenn Insurance has launched a new flood policy aimed at homes in river valleys.

The company said Kestrel & Fenn customers will be offered the cover when they renew their home insurance.

Local councillors welcomed the news but asked Kestrel & Fenn to publish prices clearly.
